Asteroid Nova

​Blast your way through deep space in this fast-paced, neon-drenched arcade shooter!

​Pilot an agile starfighter through treacherous asteroid fields, shatter incoming meteors into glowing fragments, and survive escalating waves of cosmic chaos. Built with smooth, responsive controls for both mobile touchscreens and desktop keyboards, Asteroid Nova brings classic retro arcade action into a modern neon universe.
​

Key Features:

​Smooth Analog Joystick & Keyboard Controls: Effortlessly steer with 360-degree precision and proportional thrust using the on-screen virtual joystick—or fly with Arrow Keys / WASD on desktop.
​
Epic Power-Ups: Collect Triple Spread Lasers (⚡) to unleash devastating multi-shot barrages and Hull Repairs (✚) to restore your ship's shields.

​Endless Escalating Action: Every level brings faster, denser asteroid storms. Shatter massive meteors into medium and small fragments while chasing your highest score.
​
Dynamic Neon Visuals & Synth Audio: Experience glowing particle explosions, screen-shake impacts, and retro synthesized sound effects with zero downloads required.



​How to Play:

Move / Steer: Drag the on-screen Virtual Joystick (or use WASD / Arrow Keys).

​Shoot: Tap the 🔥 FIRE button (or press Spacebar).



​Launch your mission, survive the storm, and become the ultimate space ace!
